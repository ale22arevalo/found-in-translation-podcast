Hello and welcome to <b className="bolded">Found in Translation</b>. I'm your host Guru Ramanathan and this is a podcast where we talk to people who are first, second gen, or immigrants about their relationship with their cultural language and how that has influenced their relationship to their culture, family, friends, and more.

<Speaker name="Boying">
